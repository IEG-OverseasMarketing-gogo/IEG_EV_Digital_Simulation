Why Uzbekistan & Indonesia

Indonesia — ICE-majority, hybrid-first

Electrified sales (BEV+PHEV+HEV) reached roughly 15% of passenger sales by Q2 2025, up from ~10% in Q1 — meaningful but still a fast-moving, inconsistently-defined market. Toyota's own ~US$1.8B Indonesia investment plan starts with hybrid production before scaling to full battery-EV. In Indonesia this evaluation is positioned for LSP GO (a professional certification body), aligned to SKKNI No. 8/2024 EV competency mapping — not a general secondary-school rollout.

Source: ICCT, PwC Indonesia EV Readiness 2025, REGlobal, IndexBox — figures rounded, directional.

Vocational training centers are actively upgrading curricula to cover HV-vehicle safety and diagnostic judgment alongside long-standing ICE expertise. The bridge-module format in this suite is built specifically for that transition — teaching identification and verification habits before any brand-specific certification track.

Instructor's Guidance — 14-Day Evaluation Script
Day 1Kickoff & Orientation

Goal: get every learner comfortable navigating all four tools before any graded work begins.

SAY: "Over the next two weeks you'll work across four linked simulators. None of them require an account or install — bookmark this page, and you can return to any of them at any point in the trial."
Walkthrough: Open the Hybrid Drivetrain 6D Digital Twin together first — cycle through all three architectures as a group so everyone can read the four live readouts without prompting. Then open the EV Bridge Module and show the Blue tier tab structure.
CLOSE: "By tomorrow, everyone should have opened all four tools at least once. We start graded work in the Bridge Module on Day 2."
Days 2–4Blue Tier — Identification & Verification

Goal: build the identify-before-acting habit across B1–B7 in the EV Bridge Module.

SAY: "Every lesson this week asks the same underlying question: what do you actually know, versus what are you assuming? Evidence first, judgment second."
Walkthrough: Run one Blue-tier lesson together each day (B1 vehicle intake, B2 headlight circuit, B3 cable routing, or the B4 electrical-accident-response scenario), then have learners complete the remaining lessons individually. Note each mission's "Sense covered" badge out loud — it tells you exactly what this simulator does and doesn't substitute for.
CLOSE: "Log your Blue-tier results. We move to hands-on HV procedure in Orange next."
Days 5–8Orange Tier & Diagnostic Bay Bronze/Silver

Goal: apply de-energization, battery R&I, LOTO, and fluid-spec discipline — then transfer that judgment into the Diagnostic Bay's mission format.

SAY: "LOTO has no partial credit in this simulator, on purpose. Four out of five steps in the right order is not a pass — that mirrors the real consequence of getting this wrong."
Walkthrough: Complete L5–L8 in the Bridge Module. In parallel, open the EV Diagnostic Bay and clear the Bronze missions as a group, then let learners work through Silver independently, watching the mistake counter rather than just pass/fail.
CLOSE: "Everyone should be at Silver rank or higher in the Diagnostic Bay by tomorrow's session."
Days 9–12Red Tier, Gold Missions & Case Files

Goal: apply every earlier habit to higher-stakes, ambiguous judgment calls.

SAY: "Nothing new is being taught this week. You already have every tool you need — this week is about using it under more pressure and less obvious evidence."
Walkthrough: Run the Bridge Module's Battery/Motor/Inverter diagnosis lessons and the Cross-System Triangulation capstone. In the Diagnostic Bay, work through the Gold-tier Case Files as small-group exercises, discussing disagreements before submitting an answer.
CLOSE: "Bring your full results log to Day 13 — we review the whole trial together."
Day 13Review & Feedback

Goal: consolidate what was learned and surface any gaps or friction with the tools themselves.

SAY: "Today isn't about finishing content — it's about telling us, honestly, where this suite helped and where it didn't."
Walkthrough: Have each learner walk back through their Journal / results log across all four tools and name one judgment habit that changed since Day 1. Collect instructor-side notes on any content, translation, or usability issues for IEG.
Day 14Decision Day

Goal: close the evaluation and decide on next steps.

SAY: "Fourteen days ago none of you had touched this suite. Today's conversation is simple: does this belong in your program going forward, and what would need to change first?"
Follow-up: Send consolidated feedback and any renewal or licensing questions to your IEG representative.
