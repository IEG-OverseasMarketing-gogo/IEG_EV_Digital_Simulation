11-week trainer pulse

Attendance and workbook scores arrive automatically when the four trainers save their weekly workbook. The coordinator's attitude scores and comments come from the tracker sheet.

Weighted score: Attendance 50% · Submission 20% · Workbook 20% · Attitude 10% (editable on the sheet's Settings tab; this page reads those values). Attendance is Present, Late or Very late depending on how long after opening the workbook a trainer first saves the week; the Settings tab holds the minute limits. A week with no save after its closing date counts as Absent. Until the coordinator enters an attitude score, a week is scored on the other three parts.
